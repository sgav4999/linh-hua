About Us

Making insurance licensing simple.

Linh Hua is an online school built to help the next generation of insurance professionals get licensed quickly and confidently.

Our story

We started Linh Hua because too many people trying to break into insurance were stuck with outdated textbooks, confusing state requirements, and exam prep that didn't match the real test.

So we built self-paced online courses: written lessons you can also listen to read aloud, module quizzes with explanations, a full practice exam with answer review, and a printable study guide.

Everything in one place

Lessons, module quizzes, a full practice exam, and a printable study guide, all in one account.

Built for real life

Self-paced, mobile-friendly courses that fit around a full-time job or a busy schedule.
