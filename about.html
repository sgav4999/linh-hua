About Us

Making insurance licensing simple.

Linh Hua is an online school built by licensed agents to help the next generation of insurance professionals get licensed quickly and confidently.

Our story

We started Linh Hua because too many people trying to break into insurance were stuck with outdated textbooks, confusing state requirements, and exam prep that didn't match the real test.

So we built state-approved courses that actually work: clear video lessons, practice exams that mirror the real thing, and support from instructors who've been licensed agents themselves.

Today, thousands of students use Linh Hua to earn their Life and Health insurance licenses and start new careers — entirely online, at their own pace.
